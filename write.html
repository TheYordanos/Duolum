      Importing a manuscript

      You decide where every chapter begins. We never guess.

      1. Read through your text. Each paragraph of your file is shown in order.
      2. Mark where chapters start. Click “Start a chapter here” between two paragraphs. Click the break again to remove it. Everything before your first break stays in Chapter 1.
      3. Use a line as the title. If a chapter opens with a line like “Chapter One”, choose “Use next line as title” and it becomes the title instead of part of the text. You can also type a title in the Chapters list.
      4. Finish up. Press “Done splitting” and your chapters open in the editor, where you can still edit, reorder, merge or delete them.

      Mistake? Use Undo, or Clear breaks to start over. Nothing is published until you publish it.
